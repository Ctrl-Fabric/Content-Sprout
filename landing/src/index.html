Content-Sprout — Free local social post & video editor

Free open-source macOS app for social posts and short-form video. Smart crop, logos, script generator, media manager, and video editor — local-first with optional Ollama AI.

  • Batch smart crop to square, portrait, landscape, and story
  • Automatic dark / light logo placement
  • Multi-scene short-form video editor with layers and TTS
  • Script Generator, Media Manager, and Video Editor
  • Optional local models via Ollama — no cloud AI bill required
